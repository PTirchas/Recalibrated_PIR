# Analysis lineage

The working directory contained several generations of EuroLeague analysis. This public package follows the dependency chain of the current RM-PIR manuscript and excludes code that does not generate or validate its reported results.

## Included final path

1. `analytics/collect_data.py` acquires official game reports, team/player box scores, and 2025–26 play-by-play.
2. `analytics/metrics.py` supplies the official PIR and possession definitions used during dataset construction.
3. `analytics/build_datasets.py` converts cached responses into the frozen team and player inputs under `data/processed/`.
4. `analytics/rm_pir_analysis.py` is the authoritative model engine. In the working directory this implementation had been retained under an archive path even though the final technical script imported it. It is promoted here, without changing the fitted objective or reported calculations.
5. `analytics/technical_robustness.py` adds the final tie, scale, fixed-origin, club-dependence, joint-uncertainty, provenance, and player-association checks and regenerates the manuscript’s programmatic figures.
6. `tests/test_outputs.py` verifies input coverage, temporal separation, PIR reconstruction, ranks, identities, headline counts, manuscript structure, and SHA-256 hashes.

## Superseded branches not copied

- The root `scripts/` and `data_analytics/` pipelines studied correlations, clustering, networks, outliers, and generic supervised models. They belong to an earlier research question and do not produce the RM-PIR manuscript results.
- `updated_pir_approach/` and `VerificationValidation/appendix_verification/run_historical_validation.py` are VP-PIR-era precursor implementations. Their relevant model ideas and historical checks were consolidated into the final rolling engine.
- `current_paper/robustness_analyses.py` generated an earlier artifact set from the frozen precursor model. The final manuscript’s authoritative numerical record is the later `tables/`/manifest lineage generated by the rolling engine and technical robustness script.
- The other `pir_experiments/` analyses evaluate time/possession adjustment, alternative metrics, and separate team/player experiments. Only their acquisition, construction, and metric-definition components are dependencies of the final RM-PIR study.

This separation keeps the GitHub repository reproducible and reviewable without presenting exploratory or superseded results as part of the final study.
