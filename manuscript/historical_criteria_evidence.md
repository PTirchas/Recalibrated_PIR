# Historical statistics-governance evidence

This note records the documentary evidence used to assess comparability of the EuroLeague box-score fields before the currently available Statistics Criteria Manual. It supports the manuscript's historical-data limitation; it does not establish that every field definition was unchanged.

## Current criteria manual

- Euroleague Basketball, *Euroleague Basketball Statistics Criteria Manual*, version 1.2, validated 5 September 2025: <https://ftpserver.euroleague.net/general/EB_Statistics_Criteria_Manual.pdf>
- Manuscript use: direct field-definition source for the current scoring framework. The document states that its criteria apply from the 2024--25 season.

- Relevant evidence:
  - the contents identify Appendix III as the “Unified Scorers Manual for Euroleague Basketball Competitions”;
  - Article 133.8 requires unified scorers to follow that appendix and the FIBA Official Basketball Statisticians' Manual, while permitting Euroleague Basketball to establish additional criteria and data;

### FIBA reference manual

- FIBA, *FIBA Statisticians' Manual 2024*: <https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-fiba-statisticians-manual-2024.pdf>
- FIBA statisticians portal: <https://about.fiba.basketball/en/fiba-game-officials/statisticians>
- Manuscript use: identifies the external manual explicitly incorporated by reference in the archived EuroLeague bylaws. The 2024 version is not assumed to reproduce every earlier edition.

## What the documents establish

The recovered bylaws establish that at least the 2021--22 and 2022--23 seasons operated under a documented EuroLeague scorer framework, incorporated a FIBA statisticians' manual, required scorer assessment, and provided a correction procedure. This is materially stronger than relying only on observed distribution continuity.

## What remains unverified

- A season-specific Euroleague Basketball Statistics Criteria Manual or complete definition-change log has not been located for 2020--21 through 2023--24.
- The archived bylaws do not demonstrate field-by-field semantic invariance across all six analysed seasons.
- The bylaws permit Euroleague Basketball to specify additional criteria, so incorporation of the FIBA manual cannot by itself prove complete equivalence.
- Arithmetic reconstruction of official PIR and modest adjacent-season distribution shifts cannot resolve undocumented changes in recording interpretation.

The manuscript therefore states historical comparability as an assumption supported by governance evidence and empirical continuity checks, not as a verified fact. A documented future definition change should restart the eligible calibration window.
